Hangar privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Hangar is a Windows desktop app. This page says what it does with your data.

In short

Hangar has no servers of its own. What you keep in it stays on your computer and, if you sign in with Google, in your own Google Drive. The developer receives none of it. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and Hangar has no analytics or tracking.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional; it turns on settings sync. Hangar asks Google for only these permissions:

  • Your email address (openid, email) - to show which account you are signed in with.
  • Its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) - a hidden folder that only Hangar can use. Hangar cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder does not appear in your Drive.

Hangar keeps one file there, hangar-sync.zip: your saved database connections, settings, Notes, Remote Desktop sites and credentials, API Explorer collections and environments, query history and your Query Library. Passwords and keys in it are encrypted on your computer with a sync passphrase only you know (AES-256) before they are uploaded; Hangar cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

The data travels only between your computer and Google, over HTTPS. Hangar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to provide settings sync to you, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

On your computer, Google's sign-in token is kept encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI), in HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Hangar.

Stopping and deleting

  • Sign Out (Hangar: About > Account) withdraws Hangar's access at Google and forgets the token and passphrase on that computer.
  • You can also remove Hangar's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the synced file: in Google Drive, Settings > Manage apps > Hangar > Options > Delete hidden app data.
  • On a computer, Hangar's own files are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hangar and its settings in HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Hangar; delete them to remove everything there.

Other services Hangar contacts

  • Updates - Hangar asks GitHub (github.com) for its latest release, at most once a day, and downloads new versions from there. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • Ask Claude (only if you use it, with your own Anthropic API key) - your question and the database structure Hangar looks up for it go to Anthropic's API.
  • Your databases, servers and APIs - Hangar connects to the ones you set up, as you ask it to.
